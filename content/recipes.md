about formatting, mood tags, or nutrition numbers — just describe it the way
you'd tell a friend.

(Terminal: `cd ~/Projects && claude`):

  "Read content/recipes.md, and add any recipes that aren't already in
  src/data/recipes.js. Follow the existing format, and fill in reasonable
  mood, diet, and nutrition tags for anything I didn't specify."

## Example: Sunday pot roast

- Vibe: comforting, a bit of a project, great for a lazy Sunday
- Time: about 3.5 hours, mostly hands-off
- Serves: 6
- Diet notes: gluten-free if you skip the flour dredge
- Ingredients: chuck roast, carrots, potatoes, onion, beef broth, thyme, garlic
- Steps:
  1. Sear the roast on all sides in a Dutch oven.
  2. Add vegetables, broth, and herbs.
  3. Braise low and slow, 3+ hours, until fork-tender.
- Goes well with: crusty bread, a simple green salad

## Chicken with Lemons, Olives and Potatoes

- Vibe: savory, bright, cozy, easy weeknight favorite
- Time: about 1 hour total
- Serves: 4
- Diet notes: gluten-free, dairy-free
- Ingredients: chicken thighs or breasts, baby potatoes, lemons, green or black olives, garlic, olive oil, oregano, parsley, salt, black pepper
- Steps:
  1. Heat the oven to 425°F and toss the potatoes with olive oil, salt, pepper, and a little oregano.
  2. Roast the potatoes for about 15 minutes to get them started.
  3. Season the chicken well with salt, pepper, oregano, and smashed garlic.
  4. Set the chicken into the pan with the potatoes, add lemon wedges and olives, and roast until the chicken is golden and cooked through, about 30–35 minutes more.
  5. Spoon the pan juices over everything and finish with chopped parsley and an extra squeeze of lemon before serving.
- Goes well with: crusty bread, a simple green salad, or roasted green beans
